How the 36 hours unfold

A tight run of show, from check-in to the final demo. Times shown are local.

DAY 1 · 09:00
Check-in & opening ceremony
Badge pickup, team formation table, and a short kickoff on rules and judging.
DAY 1 · 10:30
Hacking begins
Clock starts. Mentors are on the floor for the full 36 hours.
DAY 1 · 20:00
Midpoint check-in
Optional progress showcase — get quick feedback from mentors before the final push.
DAY 2 · 21:00
Submissions close
Push your final commit and submit your demo link. No late entries.
DAY 2 · 22:00
Judging & live scoreboard
Top teams present on the main stage. Results announced the same night.
